Bus et Tram Nantes is a fast, offline-first companion for the Naolib public
transport network in Nantes, France (bus, tram and busway).

It is an independent, community-built alternative to the official Naolib app,
focused on speed, a clean interface and working without a connection whenever
possible.

Features:

* Interactive map of every Naolib line and stop, with lines drawn in their real
  colours and de-overlapped where several routes share the road.
* Unified search across stops and addresses.
* Stop detail with all lines and directions and the next real-time departures.
* Favourites: save a whole stop, a single line, or a specific line + direction.
* A home-screen widget showing the next departures for a favourite stop.
* Itinerary planning between two points, computed on the phone.
* Offline-first: stops, lines and timetables (Nantes Métropole open-data GTFS)
  are bundled with the app and updated automatically, so the map, search,
  timetables and itineraries work without a connection.

Real-time departures come from the open Okina feed; the app contains no ads,
no tracking and no analytics.

This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Semitan or
Naolib.
